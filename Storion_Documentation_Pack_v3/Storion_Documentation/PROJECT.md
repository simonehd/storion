# Storion

## Product vision

Storion is a global marketplace connecting stories with opportunities, with African storytelling at its heart.

Its three core promises are:

- **Discover**
- **Connect**
- **Progress**

Storion is made in Africa, where the idea began, but it is built for a global community. It should help stories and creative talent from underserved countries gain visibility, while welcoming stories, creators, industry professionals and companies from around the world.

## Why Storion exists

Storion aims to:

1. **Boost underserved countries' stories:** give stories from countries that are often overlooked greater visibility and access to opportunity.
2. **Create opportunities:** help people share stories, bring them to life and connect with people who can help make that happen.
3. **Broaden cultural knowledge and awareness:** introduce people around the world to cultures, histories and perspectives they may not otherwise encounter.
4. **Create inclusivity and representation:** encourage wider, more accurate representation of people and cultures on the global stage.

## Product positioning

Storion is not an African-only platform and is not simply a film-streaming or film-discovery service. It is a marketplace that connects stories and projects with people and opportunities that can help those stories progress.

The platform has African roots and a global outlook. Its identity should communicate where it began without suggesting that participation or content is limited to Africa.

The approved footer positioning in the supplied design reference is:

> A global platform for storytellers, with African roots.

The footer also carries a **“Made in Africa”** origin note. This is a brand-origin signature, not a restriction on the platform's audience, creators or content. It communicates where Storion began while reinforcing its global outlook.

## Core experience

- **Discover:** explore stories, projects, creators, companies and cultures.
- **Connect:** find relevant people and opportunities, and express interest.
- **Progress:** manage interest and submissions and follow their status.

AI-powered matching supports discovery and connection by identifying potentially relevant opportunities and explaining why they may be relevant.

## Country Spotlight

Country Spotlight is a cultural-discovery feature. It highlights a country, its culture, creators and stories, and helps users discover perspectives they may not know.

The initial emphasis is on African countries and underserved stories. The concept should be extensible to countries from other parts of the world. It must not turn Storion into an Africa-exclusive platform.
